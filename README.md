# Yumeng Chen

I build AI applications around decisions I know from hands-on work. In property management, a tenant asking for a repair reminder and a supplier asking for payment can land in the same inbox. I map what should happen next, what data the system needs, and who should make the decision. I use AI coding tools to turn those designs into working software.

My background is in data science at Western University and mathematical statistics at the University of Ottawa. I’m interested in AI application development and business systems roles where I can take a process from a conversation with its users to a working tool.

## Selected work

- **[Property operations](https://github.com/CSTGKaeson/CSTGKaeson/blob/main/property-workflow.md)** · I built the company website and a maintenance platform. For the internal AI system now in development, I design the workflows and data relationships. The case study shows where a repair reminder can be automated and where a person needs to review a financial decision.
- **[Future](https://github.com/CSTGKaeson/CSTGKaeson/blob/main/selected-work.md#future)** · A local AI workspace for my notes and conversations. If it reads a preference incorrectly, I can correct the interpretation, keep the original passage, and use the correction in later searches. Built with Python, SQLite and Ollama.
- **[NewsCollection](https://github.com/CSTGKaeson/CSTGKaeson/blob/main/selected-work.md#newscollection)** · A research workflow for following U.S. and Canadian companies. It connects news and filings to events, evidence and research hypotheses, so I can revisit what I believed before a new filing and see what changed.

For a public code sample, [Radon Operations](https://github.com/CSTGKaeson/radon-operations-app) shows the TypeScript and PostgreSQL implementation of residential test orders, kit activation and reminders, along with workflow tests.
