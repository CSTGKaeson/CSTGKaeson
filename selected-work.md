# Selected Work

## Future

I wanted to ask questions about old notes and still see which words were mine. I use AI coding tools to build the prototype around that requirement. Future uses Python, SQLite and a local model to keep each source passage alongside its interpretations and corrections.

**Illustrative example.** A note says “I’m considering a course,” but a model summary says “enrolled.” I can correct the summary and keep the original passage. To check the result, I would ask “Which courses have I signed up for?” and inspect the cited evidence. A good answer should preserve the uncertainty in the note.

## NewsCollection

I wanted to follow what changed in a company over several news cycles. NewsCollection uses Python and SQLite to collect news and filings, check for duplicate articles, and organize related coverage into events. Its AI-assisted research workflow links hypotheses to their supporting evidence and retains earlier revisions.

**Illustrative example.** An earlier assessment assumes a product will ship in June. The company later announces a delay. A reviewer can compare the original hypothesis with the revised assessment, open the supporting source, and decide whether the delay changes the outlook. That history lets me revisit what was knowable at the time.
